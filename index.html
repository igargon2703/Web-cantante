BIOGRAPHY

FROM CAGUAS TO THE WORLD

Eladio Carrión, born on November 14, 1994, in Caguas, Puerto Rico, is one of the most influential artists in the new generation of urban music.

His passion for music started at a young age, influenced by the sounds of his island and the culture of the streets. With a unique style and versatility, he has managed to build a solid career as a rapper, songwriter and producer.

Today, Eladio continues to break barriers, combining trap, reggaeton and Latin sounds, becoming a global reference in the urban scene.
